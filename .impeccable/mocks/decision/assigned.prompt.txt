Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: desktop settings application concept screenshot for scrnsvr, 1280x800 landscape.
Primary request: Design a complete, high-fidelity settings interface for a Linux WebGL screensaver. Choosing and previewing a shader must be the easiest task. The visual direction is a projectionist's illuminated slide sorting table, translated into a contemporary usable software interface.
Scene/backdrop: flat, straight-on application screenshot only, edge-to-edge pixels. No computer device, room, hands, perspective, or photo of a screen.
Composition: a thin top application bar with “scrnsvr” and “Settings”; beneath it, a three-zone working surface. Left, a clearly visible vertical contact sheet of multiple distinct colorful abstract shader thumbnails with readable names such as “Flow Field”, “Aurora Veil”, “Plasma”, and “Silk Ribbons”; selected thumbnail visibly marked. Center, a dominant large, vivid live preview of the selected abstract shader, with its name, “Live preview”, and one concise description. Right, a compact inspector with “Shader” and “Clock” tabs, a few precise labeled sliders and input values, with Randomize and preset actions. A low utility strip may hint at rotation and global settings. Gallery and preview both visible without scrolling.
Visual system: milky off-white illuminated table #e9e9df, graphite anodized metal #292d2d, restrained acid chartreuse #c6d653 for selection, translucent slide film frames. The preview and thumbnails supply most saturated color. Exact alignment rail connects selected slide, preview title, and controls; generous negative space and restrained, precise hardware details. Refined typography, readable labels, elegant contrast. Sophisticated, production-quality application UI, not a nostalgic prop. Avoid glassmorphism, generic rounded card pile, neon halos, gimmick icons, and decorative text. All UI text should be coherent and correctly spelled; no claims, features, or metrics beyond the product description.
